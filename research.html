Research

My research sits at the intersections of higher education, student success, AI in education, and international higher education, with attention to inclusive learning, career development, and decolonial approaches.

Autistic College Student Success and Career Development

This line of work examines autistic college students’ experiences of success, identity formation, and career development. It draws on research and projects focused on supporting autistic collegians in navigating academic and professional pathways with attention to inclusive student support and institutional practice.

AI, Decolonial Pedagogy, and International Higher Education

This research explores the implications of generative artificial intelligence for teaching, learning, and curriculum, with a focus on equity, decolonial pedagogy, and adult education. It also considers international higher education, career services, and the role of AI in shaping educational access and opportunity.
